# Third-party footage

## Real laparoscopy clip (demo only)

* **Title:** Supplementary video 1 to "A gallbladder torsion presenting as acute cholecystitis in an
  elderly woman: a case report"
* **Authors:** Boer J, Boerma D, de Vries Reilingh T
* **Published in:** *Journal of Medical Case Reports* 5:588 (2011). doi:[10.1186/1752-1947-5-588](https://doi.org/10.1186/1752-1947-5-588)
* **Obtained from:** Wikimedia Commons,
  [File:A-gallbladder-torsion-presenting-as-acute-cholecystitis-in-an-elderly-woman-A-case-report-1752-1947-5-588-S1.ogv](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:A-gallbladder-torsion-presenting-as-acute-cholecystitis-in-an-elderly-woman-A-case-report-1752-1947-5-588-S1.ogv)
* **License:** [Creative Commons Attribution 2.0 Generic (CC BY 2.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/)
* **Changes made:** trimmed, re-encoded, and processed with this project's enhancement pipeline to
  produce the "after" view. The "before" view is the re-encoded original.

The clip is not committed to the repository. `scripts/fetch_sample.sh` downloads it from Wikimedia
Commons. It is used only for the demo and visual review, never for pass/fail verification.

## Synthetic footage
